# Product

## Register

product

## Users
OpenGlass Hub serves AR / AI glasses users, developers, early adopters, buyers doing research, and community builders. They usually arrive with a practical question: what device is worth trying, what real users are experiencing, what current limitations exist, or where to discuss a specific product, circle, or development path.

## Product Purpose
OpenGlass Hub is a forum-first community product for AR / AI glasses and spatial computing. Its job is to make discussion, discovery, and practical decision-making easier: users should quickly find posts, circles, news, device references, and product-specific conversations without feeling like they landed on a generic AI tool landing page.

## Brand Personality
Practical, credible, focused. The interface should feel like a real community product with strong information hierarchy, dark premium restraint, and zero fake hype. It should feel closer to a trusted forum or developer product than to a marketing-heavy SaaS homepage.

## Anti-references
Do not make this look like a generic AI startup landing page, a blue SaaS dashboard, or a concept-heavy productivity template. Avoid fake metrics, oversized hero marketing, decorative glass layers without purpose, and empty product language that hides the real community content.

## Design Principles
1. Put discussion before promotion.
2. Make community navigation obvious within one glance.
3. Keep dark surfaces restrained, dense, and readable.
4. Let real content carry the page instead of decorative scaffolding.
5. Preserve familiar forum patterns over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Maintain readable contrast on all dark surfaces, keep keyboard focus visible, support reduced motion, avoid hidden state that depends on animation to appear, and ensure mobile layouts remain readable without horizontal overflow. Public profile, forum, and search flows must remain stable for logged-out users as well as authenticated users.
