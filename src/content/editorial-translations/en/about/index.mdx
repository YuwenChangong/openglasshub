## A structured reference

OpenGlass Hub brings together product information, buying considerations, developer resources and community observations about AR and AI glasses. The project was initiated and is maintained by Gaze.

Its purpose is to make evidence easier to compare. A specification, a manufacturer's statement, an independent observation and a proposed feature are different kinds of information; they should not be presented as interchangeable proof.

## Editorial boundaries

The reference collection is not a product ranking or a promise that a device will support a particular workflow. Unverified information should remain identified as unverified. Gaze Launcher material should be distinguished from information about third-party products, and an experimental direction should not be presented as a completed hardware integration.

The source editorial policy states that the project does not sell products, include commerce links or accept paid brand promotion. Those policy statements are part of the editorial review of this English edition, not a certification of every external resource linked by the site.

## Sources and corrections

Manufacturer specifications and developer documentation provide starting points. Published device observations and reproducible development experiments can add context, but the limits of that evidence still matter.

Corrections should identify the affected page, explain the discrepancy and provide a source or a reproducible observation. Visit the [community](/feed/) to discuss findings and the [contact page](/contact/) for the site's public contact channels.

Original-language articles can remain available when no reviewed English edition exists. Interface language and document language are separate choices; an untranslated source document should be labelled honestly rather than silently replaced.
