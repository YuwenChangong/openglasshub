## Establish the supported development path

Begin with the device's documentation, not a general category label. Record which hardware and software version you are examining, the supported build tools, the installation method and the permissions available to third-party applications.

Distinguish a display connected to another device from a platform that runs your application. Neither a product demonstration nor the existence of an SDK establishes access to every sensor or system service.

Check the current documentation for the exact product before depending on any stated capability. Do not transfer a platform claim to another device merely because the brand is the same.

## Capture reproducible evidence

For an experiment, record the device and runtime versions, input method, granted permissions, installation steps and observed result. Separate tested behavior from plans, assumptions and capabilities that remain unavailable or unverified.

Start with the [development limits checklist](/guides/developer-limit-checklist-before-choosing-ar-ai-glasses/) and bring reproducible observations to the [forum](/feed/). This page does not announce a public Gaze Launcher SDK or certify hardware compatibility.
